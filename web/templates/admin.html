Universal Calendar & Mail Ecosystems

FamilyBoard seamlessly unifies Google Workspace, Microsoft Exchange, and Apple iCloud into one real-time family kiosk.

🌐 Google Workspace & Gmail

Supports personal @gmail.com and custom Google Workspace domains with automatic Google Meet link integration and secondary group calendar discovery.

G Suite Tip: If 3rd-party access is restricted in your domain, go to Google Admin Console > Security > API Controls to trust FamilyBoard.

🏢 Microsoft Exchange & 365

Syncs Outlook, Hotmail, and work/school Exchange Online accounts with high-performance delta tokens, Teams meeting links, and conflict locking.

Exchange Tip: For enterprise tenants blocking consumer multi-tenant apps, configure your tenant_id in config.toml.

🍏 Apple iCloud & CalDAV

Connect via shared Public Calendar links (webcal://) with zero passwords, or Apple ID + App-Specific Password for bidirectional sync and FaceTime links.

Apple Tip: Generate an App-Specific Password at appleid.apple.com for full two-way editing.
